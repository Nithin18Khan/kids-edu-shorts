## Rules

1. Prefer **CC0** or **CC-BY** with credit line in video description.  
2. Reject assets with “editorial only” or unclear ToS.  
3. Prefer stylized / toy-like over hyper-real gore anatomy for ages under 11.  
4. One episode should pull a **different combination** of props when possible.  
5. Episode JSON may still list `PLACEHOLDER_*.md` until a real `.glb` / `.blend` is dropped here. The renderer skips those files.
